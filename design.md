This section showcases my visual design and science communication work, focusing on **conference branding**, **promotional media**, and **scientific outreach**.

  <h1>EMPSEB29 — Visual Identity</h1>
  <em>(Conference branding and communication suite)</em>

  <p>
  As part of the organizing team for the 29th European Meeting of PhD Students in Evolutionary Biology (<strong>EMPSEB29</strong>),
  I developed the full visual identity for the conference — from the logo and social-media materials to printed items and sponsor packages.
  </p>
