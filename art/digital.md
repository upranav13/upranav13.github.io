<p>
Digital art has become a space where I experiment with composition, lighting, and design across different illustration styles.  
These works range from stylized chibi drawings and anatomy-focused character pieces to full vector illustrations and pixel art.  
Each project is a small exploration of how digital tools can merge technical precision with storytelling.
</p>

<hr>

<h1>🎀 Chibi Art</h1>
<p>
These drawings were inspired by the chibi art style—simplified, expressive characters with exaggerated features.  
I enjoy the freedom that comes with chibi design, where personality can shine through minimal detail.  
Both of these pieces were drawn digitally with a focus on playful poses, clear linework, and vibrant background gradients.  
They capture the energy and charm of stylized illustration while keeping a soft, hand-drawn appeal.
</p>

<h1>🧍‍♀️ Character & Anatomy Studies</h1>
<p>
These character drawings focus on anatomy, form, and digital colouring practice.  
Here, I worked on proportions, gesture flow, and clothing folds—balancing stylized design with semi-realistic lighting.  
Both characters reflect different moods and tones: one bold and confident, the other calm and composed.  
Through these, I practiced layer-based blending and brush pressure control to refine my linework and shading.
</p>

<h1>🏡 Vector Illustration</h1>
<p>
This piece was built entirely with vector graphics, from shape layering to light gradients and texture effects.  
I focused on environmental storytelling—using stylized forms and colours to create a whimsical yet structured composition.  
Vector-based work allows for crisp precision and scalability while maintaining a soft, storybook-like charm.  
This strawberry cottage scene was my exploration into background and environmental design in a stylized world.
</p>

<h1>🌸 Pixel Art</h1>
<p>
This digital piece was made in pixel format, where every detail depends on colour placement and restraint.  
It represents a blooming sakura tree in the rain, created pixel by pixel to evoke a gentle, atmospheric feeling.  
Pixel art helps refine my understanding of lighting, composition, and visual economy—how to convey more with less.  
The simplicity of this medium makes it one of my favourite forms of creative relaxation.
</p>
