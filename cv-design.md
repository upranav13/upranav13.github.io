## 🎨 Design & Communication

My design work spans **scientific communication and academic event design**, including conference branding, scientific figures and illustrations, infographics, posters, presentations, and digital communication materials. Across these different formats, I focus on presenting information clearly while maintaining visual consistency and an appropriate identity for the project or audience.

---

## 🧭 EMPSEB29 — Conference Organization, Design & Communication (2024)

I was part of the organizing team for the **29th European Meeting of PhD Students in Evolutionary Biology (EMPSEB29)**, held in **Puchberg am Schneeberg, Austria**. My role combined conference organization with responsibility for much of the meeting's visual identity and communication.

### ✦ Design & Communication Responsibilities

I developed and coordinated the visual identity and a range of communication materials for EMPSEB29, including:

- The **official conference logo** and broader visual identity
- A suite of **social-media templates** for conference announcements and updates
- Branded **participant ID cards**, **certificates**, and **sponsorship materials**
- Large-format **conference banners** and other on-site materials
- **Opening and closing ceremony presentations** and other presentation materials
- Visual content for participant, sponsor, and conference communication

The materials followed a consistent colour palette, typography, and visual style across digital and printed formats.

### ✦ Organization & Coordination

Alongside the design work, I contributed to the broader organization of the conference, including **sponsorship coordination, finances, contracts, communication with partners, and event planning**. Working as part of an international organizing team required coordinating different responsibilities while keeping communication and conference materials consistent throughout the planning process.

The EMPSEB29 branding was later recognized by participants and sponsors for its clarity, accessibility, and attention to visual detail.

## 📢 Polish Evolutionary Conference (PEC 2023) — Volunteer, Communications Support & Presenter

At the **Polish Evolutionary Conference 2023**, I contributed as a volunteer while also participating as a research presenter. I supported the organizing team with **conference logistics, participant communication, and social-media updates**, alongside delivering both an oral presentation and a poster on my doctoral research.

This provided further experience with the communication and organizational work involved in running a scientific meeting, while allowing me to participate in the conference from both an organizational and research perspective.

## 🧬 Science Communication & Visual Design

Beyond conference branding, I use illustration and design as tools for **scientific communication**. My work includes:

- **Scientific illustrations** of organisms, biological structures, and research concepts
- **Experimental schematics** and visual representations of research workflows
- **Infographics** explaining scientific papers and evolutionary concepts
- **Research posters** combining scientific results with structured visual presentation
- **Presentation layouts** for talks, research projects, and thesis presentations
- **Educational and outreach visuals** for communicating science to broader audiences

I also use these skills when helping students and colleagues improve scientific figures, posters, and presentations, with an emphasis on clear organization and effective visual communication.

## 🤝 Organizational & Team Experience

My organizational experience extends beyond visual design and includes coordinating academic, outreach, and student-led events.

As part of the **EMPSEB29 Organising Committee**, I worked with an international team on conference planning, sponsorship, finances, partner communication, visual materials, and event coordination.

During my time at **IISER Tirupati**, I contributed to science outreach and academic events, including **National Science Day activities, school outreach, workshops, and student-led events**. I also served as a **Lead Organizer for Vivante**, a university festival involving cultural, scientific, and creative activities, where I contributed to logistics, scheduling, communication, publicity, and coordination between teams.

These experiences have involved working across creative and organizational responsibilities, from developing visual materials to coordinating people, schedules, and event logistics.

## 🧩 Design Tools & Workflow

My workflow combines both **creative design** and **technical precision**, using tools such as:
- **Krita**, **Canva**, and **Adobe Photoshop** for digital illustration and layout design  
- **Inkscape** for vector graphics and logo design  
- **QGIS**, **R (ggplot2)**, and **Python (matplotlib)** for data-driven visuals  
- **LaTeX** and **Markdown** for reproducible, clean layouts  
- **Jekyll** and **GitHub Pages** for web presentation and digital portfolios  

Every project begins with sketches or wireframes, followed by digital composition, palette testing, and iterative feedback cycles to ensure accessibility and consistency across formats.
