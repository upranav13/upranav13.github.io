## 🎨 Design & Communication

My design work focuses on creating cohesive visual identities for academic events and scientific initiatives.  
I approach each project as a storytelling challenge — transforming scientific and organizational goals into clear, memorable, and versatile visuals.  
Whether designing conference materials or conceptual science-art pieces, I aim to connect content, audience, and aesthetics through clean, intentional design.

---

## 🧭 EMPSEB29 — Visual Identity & Communication Lead (2024)

I served as the **Communication and Design Lead** for the *29th European Meeting of PhD Students in Evolutionary Biology (EMPSEB29)*, hosted in Kraków.  
This international student-led conference brought together over 100 participants from across Europe, and I was responsible for its complete visual and digital communication design.

### ✦ Core Responsibilities
I developed and coordinated the entire **visual identity** for EMPSEB29 — including:
- The **official conference logo** and icon system, designed to reflect both unity and individuality in evolution.  
- A full suite of **social-media templates** for Twitter/X, Instagram, and email banners.  
- Branded **participant ID cards**, **certificates**, and **sponsorship materials**.  
- Large-format **conference banners** and **poster templates** for on-site displays.  
- **Opening and closing ceremony presentations**, designed for clarity, flow, and visual cohesion with the EMPSEB29 theme.  

All materials followed a unified colour palette, typography set, and visual hierarchy for recognizability and consistency across platforms.

### ✦ Communication & Outreach
In addition to design, I co-managed EMPSEB29’s digital communication strategy — handling **social-media promotion**, **announcement scheduling**, and **email campaigns** for participants and sponsors.  
This involved maintaining a coherent visual and narrative tone, combining professional communication with a welcoming student-driven identity.  

The EMPSEB29 branding was later recognized by participants and sponsors for its clarity, accessibility, and attention to visual detail.

## 🧬 Science Communication & Design Integration

My ongoing projects focus on:
- Creating visual metaphors for evolutionary and ecological concepts.  
- Developing infographic-style figures for research dissemination.

These efforts also influence my teaching — I often help students and colleagues refine poster layouts and figures, emphasizing clarity, typography, and the balance between information and whitespace.

## 🧩 Design Tools & Workflow

My workflow combines both **creative design** and **technical precision**, using tools such as:
- **Krita**, **Canva**, and **Adobe Photoshop** for digital illustration and layout design  
- **Inkscape** for vector graphics and logo design  
- **QGIS**, **R (ggplot2)**, and **Python (matplotlib)** for data-driven visuals  
- **LaTeX** and **Markdown** for reproducible, clean layouts  
- **Jekyll** and **GitHub Pages** for web presentation and digital portfolios  

Every project begins with sketches or wireframes, followed by digital composition, palette testing, and iterative feedback cycles to ensure accessibility and consistency across formats.

## ✨ Design Philosophy

Design, for me, is an act of communication — a dialogue between the visual and the conceptual.  
I approach every project with curiosity and adaptability, combining precision with creativity to craft visuals that inform, invite, and inspire.  
My aim is to bring the same sense of aesthetic clarity and integrative thinking to scientific spaces that art brings to storytelling.
