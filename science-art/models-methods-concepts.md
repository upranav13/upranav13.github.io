## Models, Methods, and Concepts in Science Art

This collection brings together **scientific illustrations**, **concept-based visualizations**, and **design elements** inspired by research methods.  
It reflects how I merge scientific observation with creative interpretation — from model-species drawings to simplified icons that communicate complex ideas visually.

---
